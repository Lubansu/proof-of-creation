## 🇫🇷 France

### Automatic protection
Yes — **Code de la Propriété Intellectuelle (CPI), Art. L111-1**.
Protection exists from the moment of creation. No registration needed
for the right to exist.

### Recommended deposits

| Option | Authority | Cost | Validity | Best for |
|--------|-----------|------|----------|----------|
| Enveloppe Soleau | INPI | €15 | 5 ans (renouvelable) | Individus, petits projets |
| Dépôt APP | APP | €50–€150 | Permanent | Logiciels commerciaux |
| Acte notarié | Notaire | €100–€300 | Permanent | Projets haute valeur |
| Huissier de justice | Huissier | €80–€200 | Permanent | Preuve maximale |

### Practical steps — Enveloppe Soleau
1. Go to [inpi.fr](https://www.inpi.fr)
2. Create account → "Dépôts" → "Enveloppe Soleau"
3. Upload a ZIP of your source code (max 10MB)
4. Pay €15 by card
5. Receive your deposit certificate by email
6. Renew after 5 years if needed

### Duration
Life of author + 70 years (all heirs).

### Special notes
- **RGPD/GDPR**: If your app processes EU personal data, CNIL compliance is
  mandatory regardless of company size. DPO required above certain thresholds.
- **AI Act (2026)**: Content from Claude models post-August 2026 carries
  mandatory EU watermarks. Your copyright is not affected, but keep your
  creative direction documented (prompts, iterations, design decisions).
- **Freelancers**: Enveloppe Soleau is sufficient for client work disputes.
  Always include IP clauses in your contracts.

## 🇪🇺 European Union (general)

### Automatic protection
Yes — **EU Directive 2009/24/EC** on the legal protection of computer programs.
Applies uniformly across all 27 member states.

### Key points
- No EU-level copyright registration exists (each country manages its own)
- Copyright is automatic, immediate, and free
- Moral rights (right of attribution) cannot be waived in most EU countries

### EU AI Act — August 2026 implications
- AI-generated content from compliant models carries machine-readable watermarks
- This does NOT negate your copyright
- The watermark proves Claude was involved; it does not determine authorship
- Keep documentation of your creative process: prompts, decisions, iterations

### Cross-border enforcement
- EU copyright judgments are generally enforceable across member states
- For disputes exceeding €10,000, consider specialist IP counsel

## 🇺🇸 United States

### Automatic protection
Yes — **17 U.S.C. § 102**. However, **registration at the Copyright Office is
required to sue for statutory damages and attorney's fees.**

Without registration, you can only recover actual damages (very hard to prove
for software). With registration (before infringement or within 3 months of
publication), you can recover up to $150,000 per work plus attorney's fees.

### Registration

| Option | Cost | Processing | Notes |
|--------|------|-----------|-------|
| Online (single work) | $65 | 3–11 months | Standard |
| Online (group) | $85 | 3–11 months | Up to 10 works |
| Paper filing | $125 | 8–12 months | Avoid if possible |
| Expedited (special handling) | $800 | 5 business days | Urgent litigation |

**URL:** [copyright.gov/registration](https://www.copyright.gov/registration/)

### Practical steps
1. Go to [copyright.gov](https://copyright.gov)
2. Create eCO account
3. "Register a Work" → "Computer File"
4. Upload source code as ZIP (redact trade secrets if needed)
5. Pay $65
6. Receive registration certificate (retroactive to filing date)

### Duration
- Individual: Life + 70 years
- Corporate (work for hire): 95 years from publication or 120 years from creation

### AI-generated content (2026 US policy)
The USPTO and Copyright Office do not grant copyright to purely AI-generated
content. Human creative authorship must be documented and demonstrable.
Keep records of your creative direction: which elements you designed,
specified, or directed.

### Trade secrets (alternative/complementary protection)
For proprietary algorithms, consider trade secret protection (Defend Trade
Secrets Act, 2016). Requires confidentiality measures — never open-source
trade secrets.

## 🇬🇧 United Kingdom

### Automatic protection
Yes — **Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (CDPA)**. No registration
system exists in the UK for copyright.

### Recommended approach

| Option | Cost | Notes |
|--------|------|-------|
| UK Copyright Service | Free–£70/year | Timestamped certificate |
| Git commit history | Free | Valid evidence in UK courts |
| IP Notary (online) | £20–£50 | Stronger evidentiary weight |

**URL:** [copyrightservice.co.uk](https://www.copyrightservice.co.uk)

### Duration
Life + 70 years.

### Post-Brexit notes
- EU copyright directives no longer apply automatically in the UK
- Protection level remains equivalent to EU standards
- UK courts apply CDPA independently of CJEU decisions

### Design rights
UK has unregistered design rights (automatic, 10–15 years) for original
designs. Relevant for UI/UX design work.

## 🇨🇦 Canada

### Automatic protection
Yes — **Copyright Act (R.S.C., 1985, c. C-42)**, amended 2022 to extend
duration to life + 70 years (aligned with US/EU).

### Registration (optional)

| Option | Cost | Notes |
|--------|------|-------|
| CIPO registration | $50 CAD | Creates public record |
| Formal certificate | Additional fee | Stronger proof |

**URL:** [ic.gc.ca/cipo](https://www.ic.gc.ca/eic/site/cipointernet-internetopic.nsf/eng/home)

### Duration
Life + 70 years (since 2022).

### Quebec specifics
Quebec civil law traditions may apply in some contract contexts. IP rights
themselves follow federal Copyright Act across all provinces.

## 🇩🇪 Germany

### Automatic protection
Yes — **Urheberrechtsgesetz (UrhG)**. Software protected as "Werk der Technik."

### Registration
No formal registration system. Use timestamped services:
- **DPMA** (Deutsches Patent- und Markenamt) — trademarks and patents only
- Private timestamping services recommended

### Duration
Life + 70 years.

### Key note
Germany has strong moral rights (Urheberpersönlichkeitsrecht) that
**cannot be transferred**, only licensed. Important for freelancer contracts.

## 🇨🇭 Switzerland

### Automatic protection
Yes — **Urheberrechtsgesetz (URG)**. Not an EU member but largely aligned
with EU standards.

### Registration
**IGE/IPI** (Institut Fédéral de la Propriété Intellectuelle) — CHF 200+
for trademarks. Copyright is automatic.

### Duration
Life + 70 years.

Yes — **Ley de Propiedad Intelectual (LPI)**.

Registration is optional but creates presumption of authorship.

### Duration
Life + 70 years.

Life + 70 years.

## License type decision matrix

| Situation | Recommended license | Reason |
|-----------|-------------------|--------|
| Commercial SaaS, proprietary | All Rights Reserved | Full control, no sharing obligation |
| Open-source, want max adoption | MIT | Minimal restrictions, maximum reach |
| Open-source, want derivatives open | AGPL-3.0 | Forces network services to open source |
| Library/SDK for businesses | Apache-2.0 | Patent grant included, business-friendly |
| Creative content (non-code) | CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 | Attribution required, no commercial reuse |
| Internal company tool | Proprietary | No external sharing |
| Academic/research | CC BY 4.0 or MIT | Maximum reuse for science |
| Hardware + software | CERN-OHL + MIT | Covers both layers |
